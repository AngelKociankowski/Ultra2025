## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS in one file (`landing/index.html`), separate from the Next.js internal platform in this repo. It can be hosted anywhere, including corporativoultra.com. Confirmed with the user.

## Users

Security and operations decision makers at companies that contract private security in CDMX and Estado de México: industrial parks, CEDIS and logistics, corporates, high-end residential, government and penitentiary sites, finance and retail. They pay for guard posts today and face the 48→40 hour workweek reform (DOF 03/03/2026 and 01/05/2026), which raises the cost of every 24/7 post.

## Product Purpose

The "Análisis de Optimización de Seguridad" from Corporativo Ultra. It decides post by post what to eliminate, substitute, complement or reinforce, so the site detects earlier and responds in time with fewer man-hours before the reform raises the cost. Success: the visitor books the survey (levantamiento) of a pilot site.

## Positioning

Ultra designs, operates and monitors the system and answers for the result: R.O.C.A.© methodology (Risk, Optimization, Compliance, Assessment), guards since 1989 (costs come from the payroll Ultra actually pays), its own 24/7 monitoring center staffed by Ultra, and every analysis reviewed and signed by a CPP.

## Operating Context

Six phases: levantamiento (day and night walk), risk (ISO 31000 matrix), layers (detection, delay, response times), redesign (decision per post), business case (5-year cost 2026 to 2030), compliance (CTPAT, OEA, LFT, REPSE). Deliverables: post matrix, layer design, business case, compliance map. Technology is leased as a monthly rent inside the service, renewed at the end of term at the same rent. Implementation follows the reform calendar, one stage per year to 2030.
